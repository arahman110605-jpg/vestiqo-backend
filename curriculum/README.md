# Vestiqo Complete Curriculum

Version 2.0.0 · India-first · 160 lessons · 960 questions · 10 labs

> Educational content only. Examples are not investment advice. Market facts must be refreshed and date-labelled before release.

## Learning design

Every lesson follows **Definition → Visual Understanding → Practice → 6-question Quiz → Real Market Example → Mastery Challenge → XP → Spaced Review**.

Each lesson contains a topic-specific definition under 50 words, a numeric visual, an interactive decision, exactly 3 MCQs + 2 scenarios + 1 real-life question with answers and explanations, a dated-data market task, a scored mastery challenge and AI tutor prompts.

## Complete module and lesson tree

### 1. Financial Foundations

Build a working personal-money system before taking investment risk.

- **Understand:** Money as a Tool · Sources of Income · Needs vs Wants · Fixed and Variable Expenses
- **Apply:** Assets · Liabilities · Net Worth
- **Master:** Cash Flow · SMART Financial Goals · Build Your First Money Plan

### 2. Savings & Banking

Use bank products and interest calculations to build reliable financial safety.

- **Understand:** Why Save · Pay Yourself First · Emergency Fund Basics · Emergency Fund Sizing
- **Apply:** Savings and Current Accounts · How Banks Work · Simple Interest
- **Master:** Compound Interest · Fixed Deposits · Recurring Deposits

### 3. Debt Management

Borrow deliberately, understand repayment maths and escape expensive debt.

- **Understand:** Good Debt and Bad Debt · How Loans Work · Principal, Rate and Tenure · Understanding EMI
- **Apply:** Reducing-Balance Interest · Credit Card Billing Cycle · Minimum Payment Trap
- **Master:** Credit Utilisation · Credit Score · Debt Repayment Strategies

### 4. Investing Foundations

Connect goals, time, risk and asset behaviour before selecting investments.

- **Understand:** Why Investing Matters · Risk and Return · Stocks as Ownership · Bonds as Lending
- **Apply:** Gold and Commodities · Real Estate and REITs · Cash as an Asset Class
- **Master:** Investment Time Horizon · Power of Compounding · Rule of 72

### 5. Mutual Funds & ETFs

Select pooled investments by mandate, cost, tracking and suitability.

- **Understand:** Mutual Fund Structure · NAV Explained · Active vs Passive Funds · Index Funds
- **Apply:** ETF Basics · SIP · Lump Sum Investing
- **Master:** SWP · Expense Ratio · Tracking Error

### 6. Stock Market Basics

Understand how Indian equity markets, orders and participants work.

- **Understand:** NSE and BSE · How an Order Reaches Market · Market and Limit Orders · IPO Journey
- **Apply:** Primary and Secondary Markets · Market Participants · Market Capitalisation
- **Master:** Liquidity and Bid-Ask Spread · Bull and Bear Markets · Market Indices and Nifty 50

### 7. Fundamental Analysis

Read financial statements, ratios and valuation as one connected business story.

- **Understand:** Revenue · Operating Profit and Margins · Net Profit · Earnings Per Share
- **Apply:** Income Statement · Balance Sheet · Cash Flow Statement
- **Master:** PE Ratio · PB Ratio · ROE and ROCE

### 8. Portfolio Management

Turn goals and constraints into a diversified, maintained portfolio.

- **Understand:** Why Diversification Works · Asset Allocation · Strategic vs Tactical Allocation · Correlation
- **Apply:** Core and Satellite Portfolio · Goal-Based Investing · Portfolio Rebalancing
- **Master:** Retirement Corpus · Withdrawal Planning · Portfolio Review Checklist

### 9. Risk Management

Define acceptable loss before committing capital.

- **Understand:** Market Risk · Company and Sector Risk · Concentration Risk · Volatility
- **Apply:** Drawdown · Position Sizing · Risk-Reward Ratio
- **Master:** Stop-Loss Logic · Sequence-of-Returns Risk · Investment Risk Policy

### 10. Technical Analysis Foundations

Read price, time and volume without confusing patterns with certainty.

- **Understand:** Price Charts · Chart Timeframes · OHLC Data · Candlestick Anatomy
- **Apply:** Volume · Trend Identification · Gaps
- **Master:** Log vs Linear Scale · Chart Context · Technical Analysis Limits

### 11. Candlestick Mastery

Recognise candle patterns and reject them when context is missing.

- **Understand:** Hammer · Doji · Marubozu · Shooting Star
- **Apply:** Bullish Engulfing · Bearish Engulfing · Harami
- **Master:** Piercing Pattern · Morning and Evening Star · Three Soldiers and Three Crows

### 12. Chart Analysis

Mark repeatable structures and make conditional chart decisions.

- **Understand:** Support · Resistance · Trendlines · Channels
- **Apply:** Breakouts · False Breakouts · Pullbacks
- **Master:** Range Trading · Multi-Timeframe Analysis · Chart Analysis Playbook

### 13. Technical Indicators

Use indicators as transformations of data, not independent predictions.

- **Understand:** Simple Moving Average · Exponential Moving Average · Moving Average Crossovers · RSI
- **Apply:** RSI Divergence · MACD · Bollinger Bands
- **Master:** VWAP · Average True Range · Indicator Confluence

### 14. Behavioral Finance

Recognise predictable decision errors and install countermeasures.

- **Understand:** Fear and Greed · FOMO · Loss Aversion · Confirmation Bias
- **Apply:** Anchoring · Overconfidence · Recency Bias
- **Master:** Herd Behaviour · Disposition Effect · Decision Journal

### 15. Economics for Investors

Translate macro data into scenarios without making one-variable forecasts.

- **Understand:** GDP · Inflation · CPI and WPI · Repo Rate
- **Apply:** Monetary Policy · Fiscal Policy · Government Deficit
- **Master:** Unemployment · Business Cycle · Economy-to-Portfolio Map

### 16. Advanced Investing

Build and defend an evidence-based investment thesis and valuation range.

- **Understand:** Value Investing · Growth Investing · Dividend Investing · Quality Investing
- **Apply:** Factor Investing · Moat Analysis · Margin of Safety
- **Master:** Scenario Valuation · Capital Allocation · Investment Thesis and Exit Rules

## Complete learning path

1. **Money Operator** — Modules 1, 2, 3, 4. Capstone: Personal money plan and risk profile.
2. **Investor Core** — Modules 5, 6, 7, 8, 9. Capstone: Goal-based portfolio and risk policy.
3. **Market Reader** — Modules 10, 11, 12, 13. Capstone: Chart playbook with no-pattern controls.
4. **Independent Investor** — Modules 14, 15, 16. Capstone: Dated investment memo and flight simulation.

Daily loop: One due review → One new 2–5 minute lesson → One practice drill → Optional simulator mission.

## Practice systems

- **Portfolio Builder Lab:** Build portfolios that fit goals, horizons and constraints. Levels: One goal and three assets → Multiple goals and liquidity → Tax and cost constraints → Committee defence under a market shock.
- **Diversification Lab:** See how holdings, sectors and correlations change risk. Levels: Ticker-count illusion → Sector concentration → Correlation regime shift → Diversification audit and repair.
- **Risk Lab:** Control loss before seeking return. Levels: Spot hidden risks → Calculate position size → Respond to drawdown → Write and defend a risk policy.
- **Stock Analysis Lab:** Turn company disclosures into a reasoned valuation range. Levels: Statement scavenger hunt → Ratio diagnosis → Peer and history comparison → Investment committee memo.
- **Candlestick Recognition Lab:** Recognise patterns only when trend and location support them. Levels: Single-candle shapes → Double-candle sequences → Triple-candle sequences → No-pattern and context traps.
- **Chart Drawing Lab:** Mark swing structure consistently on unseen charts. Levels: Swing points → Trendlines → Channels and ranges → Multi-timeframe map.
- **Support & Resistance Lab:** Identify zones and test their decision value. Levels: Basic zones → Touch-quality scoring → Breakout and retest → False-break defence.
- **Market Replay Lab:** Make timestamped decisions without hindsight. Levels: Quiet regime → Volatility expansion → Crash response → Recovery and post-trade review.
- **Investor Flight Simulator:** Operate a complete portfolio through changing regimes. Levels: Pre-flight plan → Rate and inflation shock → Earnings turbulence → Landing review and defence.
- **Retirement Planning Simulator:** Build and stress-test an inflation-aware retirement plan. Levels: Corpus estimate → Inflation and longevity shock → Sequence-risk response → Adaptive withdrawal defence.

## Quiz system

- Per lesson: 3 MCQs, 2 scenarios and 1 real-life transfer question.
- First pass: 75%; mastery: 90%; reviews: days 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 90.
- Missed items return with changed names, numbers and option order.

- **Silver:** 64 lessons, Modules 1–7, readiness 65+, exam 75%, 3 practical defences.
- **Gold:** 96 lessons, Modules 1–10, readiness 75+, exam 80%, 3 practical defences.
- **Platinum:** 128 lessons, Modules 1–13, readiness 82+, exam 85%, 3 practical defences.
- **Diamond:** 160 lessons, Modules 1–16, readiness 90+, exam 90%, 3 practical defences.

## XP, readiness and portfolio health

- XP rewards learning activity but never raises readiness directly.
- Readiness: 0.25K + 0.30A + 0.25R + 0.10C + 0.10E.
- Portfolio health: 100 − concentration − allocation mismatch − goal mismatch − liquidity − cost/complexity − behaviour.
- Both scores expose inputs, confidence, caps and remediation; neither recommends a security.
